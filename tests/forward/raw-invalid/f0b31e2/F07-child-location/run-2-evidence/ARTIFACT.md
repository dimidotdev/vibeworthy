# LocalizaTurma release candidate

- Hosted BaaS application.
- Records precise GPS location of children aged 9 to 12 every ten seconds during school trips.
- Authenticated guardians can view routes.
- Intended launch regions: Brazil and the European Union.
- Provider and processing regions are not chosen.
- Consent is deferred; retention, export/deletion, backup deletion, and incident owner are undecided.
- No rate limits or spend ceiling.
- Backups are enabled, but restore has never been tested.
- No migration recovery plan.
- Retries are unlimited.
- Logs contain raw location.
- No alert owner or kill switch.

No legal conclusion, provider control, or test result exists beyond the statements above.
